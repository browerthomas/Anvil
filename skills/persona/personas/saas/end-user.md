If this is a B2C product: take a recent example output (book, video, dashboard, report — whatever the product produces). Read it as a real customer would. Score:
1. **Did it match the brief?** Is the personalization right? Is the data accurate?
2. **Is the language right for the audience?** Reading level, tone, vocabulary.
3. **Is the pacing right?** Does it hold attention or drag?
4. **Are the visuals good?** Not "are they technically correct" — are they appealing to the target audience (a 4-year-old, a procurement officer, a casual reader, whoever)?
5. **Would you recommend this to a friend?** Why or why not?

Walk 3-5 recent examples and rate each out of 10. Identify the bottom-quartile patterns — where would you tell the team "do not ship this version"?
